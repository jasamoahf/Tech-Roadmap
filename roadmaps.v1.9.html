Capstone Project
Secure Software Delivery Platform

Build the reference DevSecOps pipeline that your entire engineering org could adopt. Security gates at every stage, zero secrets in code, container supply chain integrity verified, runtime threats detected automatically — and a metrics dashboard proving the program is working.

What You'll Build
A hardened end-to-end secure delivery pipeline

From pre-commit hooks blocking secrets, through SAST/SCA gates in CI, to signed container images, IaC policy enforcement, DAST against staging, runtime Falco monitoring — and a Grafana dashboard tracking MTTR, open vulns, and pipeline pass rates.

Why It Matters
The gold standard DevSecOps portfolio piece

DevSecOps engineers are hired to build this. Walking a hiring manager through a live demo of your pipeline — showing a PR blocked by a critical CVE, a malicious image rejected by Gatekeeper — is the most compelling interview performance possible.
